# Scenes and elements: second pass (r5, the hackers reel, 1fps + 10fps)

## Every beat is a new place
- 84s, about 30 completely different sets: a computer desk, a 3D globe on a stand, an old map, a calendar, an elephant under a stack of servers, a relief map of Europe, a domed building at night, server-rack archives with a magnifier, a hacker room, an aerial night city, a temple/bank facade, a kitchen table with a phone and coffee, a grid of profile cards, a bright wooden desk, a drawer with envelopes, a payment terminal.
- Backgrounds are not reused, except for a deliberate callback: the map comes back with its state changed, the phone comes back.
- The hero object is *inside* the set (the elephant on the desk, the phone on the table), not floating over a generic corridor. The set is built around the object.
- **Two acts with different palettes:** the problem is dark and warm (tungsten, night, red), and the solution is bright daylight (light wood, a plant, white). The palette switch marks the turn in the script.

## A recurring character that drives the action
- A red cursor (the "hacker") passes through most scenes and *does things*:
  - It clicks a date on the calendar and a circle appears.
  - It touches the stack and cracks spread through it.
  - It pushes the globe, which falls off its stand.
  - It clicks the bank and the cards.
- The cursor's click is a cause, and the change in the set is the effect. That is the link between scenes, not just decoration.

## State changes within the same set
- The same composition, a different version of the image. Example: the stack of servers is whole, then **red cracks spread through it** (a crossfade to a cracked version, with a pulsing red glow), then a flash.
- Infection or ink spreads across a map or a city: a growing mask on top of the image.

## Transitions born from the elements
- **Swarm into the lens (opening):**
  - Dozens of window cards fly toward the camera.
  - One of them hits the lens and fills the frame with white blur.
  - The next scene emerges from the white (8–10 frames).
- **Knock and fall:**
  - The cursor pushes the globe.
  - The globe falls and spins, and the camera follows it.
  - It hits the camera and fills the frame (blur).
  - A cut to the next set lands on a soft frame.
- **Accent flash:**
  - The image fills with the accent color (red) and a glowing hot spot, over 2 frames.
  - Then the next scene comes in at low contrast and opens up.
- Push/dive through an object that becomes the next scene (map → relief map → aerial city).

## Titles: hot accent
- The word builds (sometimes letter by letter) in the accent color, then **cools to white** after about 0.4–0.6s.
- Only the key word stays in the accent color.
- The accent is a state, not a permanent color.

## Presenter
- Absent from most beats. He comes back at points of explanation, small at the bottom.
